Swing
Hold Space to build power. Release in the gold zone to strike. On touch, hold and release the swing button.
Club / shot
Scroll or use Q / E to change club. F cycles shot types.
Aim / power
Drag across / up and down, or use the arrow keys or WASD.
Read the shot
Click or tap the minimap to open it. Left Shift previews the flight. R resets aim. C opens the round scorecard.
Spin in flight
Hold a direction and click the course, or tap a spin arrow. Caps Lock skips to the result.
Gamepad
Aim with the right stick. Pull the left stick back, then push forward to hit. D-pad changes club, LB changes shot, RT previews the shot, LT opens the map.
